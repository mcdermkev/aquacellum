Marketplace, Fees & Buyer Protection

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Beta: Stripe is in test mode. Checkouts, holds and payouts run end to end, but no real money moves and no real card is charged. Everything below describes how payments work on the site.

1. How payments work

  • Card only, through Stripe. You enter your card on Stripe's own checkout page. We never see or store your full card number.
  • No crypto. Payments are in US dollars. They don't use a blockchain, and you don't need a wallet.
  • Held until handoff or arrival. For shipped orders, local pickup and batch orders, we hold the payment. When the handoff or arrival is confirmed, we transfer the seller's share to their Stripe account.
  • Sellers are paid through Stripe Connect. Sellers set up a Stripe Express account. Stripe collects their identity and bank details, not us.
  • Guest checkout. Some local-pickup orders can be bought without an account. We ask for your email so Stripe can send a receipt and we can email you a link to your order and pickup code.

2. Fees

WhoWhat you pay
BuyersNo platform fee. You pay a card processing fee of about 2.9% + $0.30, worked out to cover Stripe's charge. It's shown as its own line at checkout.

If we change a fee, we'll update this page before the change applies.

3. Local pickup

Your pickup code is on your order page and in your order email. At the meetup, show the code to the seller and they scan it. That confirms the handoff, completes the sale and releases payment to the seller. Only show your code once you have the animals.

4. Buyer protection for shipped orders

  • Report within 48 hours of delivery. A seller may offer a longer window, never a shorter one.
  • Include at least 2 photos and a description of the problem.
  • The seller has 24 hours to respond. After that, the claim comes to us for review.
  • The outcome is a refund, a replacement, or denied, decided for each affected item. Healthy items in the same order go through as normal.
  • While a claim is open, the seller's payout for that order is paused.

This process doesn't take away any rights you have under the law or with your card issuer.

5. Auctions

  • Bidding needs a card on file. A bid is a commitment to buy.
  • Anti-sniping: a bid in the last 2 minutes pushes the end time to 2 minutes after that bid, up to 60 minutes past the original end.
  • The winner's card is charged automatically when the lot closes. If the charge fails, the winner has 24 hours to update their card and pay, or the lot goes back to the seller.
  • The money is held until pickup, then paid to the seller.

6. Selling live animals

Sellers are responsible for what they list and ship. By listing, you agree that:

  • It's legal. You follow federal, state and local rules for the animals you sell and where you ship them. Some states restrict or ban certain species, and some species need permits. Protected species (for example under CITES) need the right paperwork, or can't be sold at all.
  • The listing is accurate. Species, size, sex, quantity, health and photos honestly describe what the buyer will get.
  • Animals are packed humanely. Use proper bags, insulation, heat or cold packs for the weather, and only carriers and services that accept live animals.
  • You follow through. Ship or hand off what you sold, and respond to claims.

Buyers are responsible for checking that they can legally keep a species where they live, and for being available to receive a shipment or meet for pickup.

We may remove listings or restrict sellers that break these rules.

Privacy & Data

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This section explains what we collect, where it's stored, who can see it, and which outside services handle it for us.

1. What we collect

  • Account: your email address (from your email or Google sign-in) and the wallet address Privy creates as your account ID.
  • Profile: display name, bio, avatar, and your XP. If you set a local zone, we save a coarse area code (a grid cell, not your address).
  • Tank logbook: tanks, livestock, water tests, care logs, notes, schedules, spawns and photos you add.
  • Marketplace: listings, orders, and payment records (amounts, fees and Stripe reference numbers, never card numbers). Shipping addresses for rates and labels. Pickup spots and times. For sellers, your Stripe account ID and the email and name used to set it up.
  • Community: posts, comments, reactions, follows, club and event activity, and direct messages.
  • Feedback: anything you send through the Feedback button, including an optional screenshot.
  • Usage: pages viewed, clicks and named actions in the app (see Cookies & Local Storage).
  • Notifications: if you turn on push notifications, your browser's push address and browser type.
  • Location, only if you use it: "Near me" on the breeders map uses your device location in your browser to center the map. Event hosts can set an event's location.
  • Technical data: the services below receive your IP address and browser details as part of normal web requests. We use IP addresses for rate limiting.

2. How we use it

  • To run the features you use: your logbook, listings, checkout, payouts, shipping labels, auctions and The Reef.
  • To send email: order confirmations, auction results and account notices. Care-streak reminders and "we miss you" emails are on by default and can be turned off in Settings → Notifications. The weekly digest is only sent if you turn it on.
  • To send push notifications, if you turn them on.
  • To answer AI requests (see below).
  • To understand how the app is used and fix problems.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to handle claims and disputes.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use advertising networks or cross-site ad trackers.

3. Where your data is stored

  • In your browser: your tank logbook is saved on your device first (in the browser's IndexedDB storage), so it works offline.
  • In your account: when you're signed in, your tanks, livestock, care logs, spawns and grow-out records sync automatically to your account in our database (Supabase), so they show up on your other devices. Some photos stay on the device where you took them.
  • In our database: profiles, listings, orders, payment records, pickup details, Reef posts and messages.
  • In file storage: photos you upload for listings, specimens and Reef posts. Only your signed-in account can add photos to your folder. Feedback screenshots are kept in a separate private folder and deleted after 90 days.
  • On a public test blockchain: see section 5.

4. Who can see what

  • Public: your profile's account address, display name, avatar, bio, XP and tier, depth score, tank and species counts, whether you accept mentees, local zone, and when you joined.
  • Private: your email, notification and privacy settings, and the rest of your profile. Only you (and our server) can read them.
  • Listings are public. Anyone can see a listing's species, price, quantity, photos, care ranges, and the seller's display name and account address. The public version of a listing never includes a location.
  • Pickup locations are shared only between the buyer and seller of that order. The exception is auctions: an auction lot shows the pickup location the seller or club enters.
  • Sellers' ship-from addresses are private. They're used only to get shipping rates and buy labels, and are never shown to buyers.
  • Uploaded photos for listings, specimens and Reef posts are stored at public web addresses, so anyone with the link can view them. Don't upload photos with personal information in them.
  • Feedback screenshots are private. Only our team can open them, through links that expire after 7 days.
  • Reef posts and comments can be seen by other people, depending on the feature and your settings. Direct messages can only be read by the people in the conversation.

5. Testnet records

Some records are also written to Base Sepolia, a public test blockchain: tank registrations (name, type, volume, and any facility, room or rack labels), specimen records (species, birth date, breeder account and parents), and listing and ownership changes. We submit these for you and cover the cost, so you don't need crypto or a wallet of your own. Test network records have no monetary value.

Anything written there is public and can't be deleted, by us or anyone else. Don't put personal information in tank names or facility labels.

Cookies & Local Storage

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Aquacellum's own code doesn't set cookies. We do save data in your browser's local storage and IndexedDB, and some services we use set their own cookies.

1. What we save in your browser

  • Your offline tank logbook: tanks, livestock, care logs, water tests, schedules and some photos.
  • Settings and preferences, such as units, display mode and dismissed notices.
  • Your cart, watchlist, saved searches and wishlist.
  • Drafts and a cache of species data, so pages load faster and work offline.

2. Analytics (PostHog)

Inside the app, PostHog records pages viewed, clicks and named actions (such as creating a tank or making a purchase). It stores an ID in a first-party cookie and local storage so it can count visits. After you sign in, events are linked to your account address. Session recording is turned off. There's no in-app switch to turn analytics off yet. Browser tracker blocking or clearing site data will stop or reset it. Our public pages outside the app don't run analytics.

3. Other services

  • Privy stores your sign-in session in your browser.
  • Stripe sets its own cookies on its checkout pages.
  • YouTube may set cookies when a species page shows an embedded video.

4. Clearing it

You can clear this data in your browser's settings, or use Settings → Clear this device in the app. Either way, the offline copy of your logbook on that device is removed. Anything already synced to your account comes back when you sign in again.

Community Guidelines

Last updated: September 29, 2026

The Reef is where fishkeepers share tank updates, join clubs, run events and message each other. These guidelines apply there, in listings and in auctions.

1. Be respectful

  • Treat everyone with basic courtesy, whatever their experience level.
  • No harassment, personal attacks, hate speech, discrimination or threats.
  • Disagreeing about care practices is fine. Make it about the fish, not the person.
  • No spam, feed flooding, or promotion unrelated to fishkeeping.

2. Keep it honest

  • No fake listings. Only list animals you have and can deliver, identified as accurately as you can.
  • No made-up lineage. Pedigree and breeding records must reflect real pairings.
  • No misleading photos. Photos should show the animal or tank you're talking about, without filters that change its color. Don't use other people's photos without credit.
  • Care tips should come from your own experience or a real source. Don't post medical advice you can't back up.

3. Content standards

  • Keep content about fishkeeping, aquatic life or Aquacellum.
  • No explicit, violent or disturbing content.
  • Nothing illegal, including illegal wildlife trade.
  • Only post content you have the right to share.

4. Marketplace and auction conduct

  • Describe species, health and care needs accurately.
  • Follow through on sales. Backing out after a buyer has paid hurts everyone.
  • Don't sell species protected by CITES or by law without the required permits.
  • Only record cash sales that really happened in person.
  • Bid only if you mean to pay. Shill bidding or bid manipulation leads to suspension.

5. AI helpers

  • Poseidon and Echo are AI helpers, not veterinarians. Don't rely on them for treatment decisions.
  • Don't try to get them to produce harmful or inappropriate content.
  • Report answers that seem harmful or wrong.

6. Clubs and events

  • Club founders and event hosts are expected to keep their spaces welcoming.
  • Events, meetups and auction nights follow these same guidelines.

7. Reporting and enforcement

To report content or behavior, email kevin@aquacellum.com with a description and, if you can, a link or screenshot.

Depending on how serious it is, we may:

  1. Warn you, for a first or minor issue.
  2. Remove content or temporarily restrict posting, selling or bidding.
  3. Suspend your account, for fraud, harassment or repeated problems.
  4. Permanently ban you, for severe or repeated violations.

We may skip steps for serious violations.

8. Updates

We'll update these guidelines as the community grows and change the "Last updated" date. Using the Service after an update means you accept the current guidelines.

The short version: be kind, be honest, keep it about fish, and look after the animals.
