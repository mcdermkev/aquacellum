How XP is earned

XP comes from keeping records in the app, for example:

  • Logging a feeding or a water change
  • Recording a water test
  • Adding a species to your collection
  • Registering a tank
  • Recording a spawn or a birth certificate
  • Listing a fish for sale

Sign in to the app and start logging your tanks. Pick a display name in Settings to appear here.
