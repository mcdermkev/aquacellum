What Echo does inside the app

In the app she sits in the corner of every screen. Tap her to chat. There she can use the records you keep.

🐠

Answers with your tanks in mind

She reads the tanks you have set up, their latest water reading, their inhabitants and your recent care log. That context is sent only along with a question you ask.

For example: Is my 20 gallon warm enough for the fish I have?

✅

Shows the numbers

Ask whether fish can live together and a card lists the shared temperature and pH, the tank size needed, temperament and the catalog's own tankmate notes, checked against your tank.

For example: Can I add German blue rams to my living room tank?

📷

Looks at a photo

Send a photo and she gives her best guesses, ranked, with a match percentage and a link to the catalog entry. A photo can only suggest, so nothing is recorded for you. Needs a signed-in account.

🧪

Plans a tank with you

Pick a tank size and water type, or start from one of your tanks, and add species. The card re-checks the whole group as you go, and she reacts to each change.

📈

Notices what changed

She mentions a change between your last two water tests, like nitrate going from 10 to 40 ppm, or a care task that has not been logged in a while. Tap it to ask her why.

📝

Logs care when you say so

Tell her a water test result or a care task and she can offer to record it, or to set up a new tank. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

For example: Log pH 7.2, ammonia 0, nitrate 20 for the shrimp tank.

You can also ask by voice and have answers read aloud, in browsers that support it.
