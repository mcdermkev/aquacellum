What Poseidon does inside the app

In the app, the same assistant sits behind the Poseidon button on every screen, and it can use the records you keep there.

Answers with your tanks in mind

It reads the tanks you have set up, their latest water reading, their inhabitants and your recent care log. That context is sent only along with a question you ask.

For example: Is my 20 gallon warm enough for the fish I have?

Logs care when you tell it to

Tell it a water test result or a care task and it can offer to record it, or to set up a new tank. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

For example: Log pH 7.2, ammonia 0, nitrate 20 for the shrimp tank.

Checks compatibility and suggests species

It compares temperature and pH ranges, tank size, adult size and temperament from the catalog against what you already keep.

For example: Can I add cherry shrimp to a tank with a betta?

Finds its way around the app

Questions like where to log a water test are answered from the app's own guide, instantly, without counting against your hourly questions.

For example: Where do I add a new fish?
