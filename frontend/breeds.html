Who keeps these fish

GG Steve Rice Fish NJ

📍 Toms River, NJ · Local pickup

Steve breeds Japanese rice fish (medaka) in outdoor tubs and a planted show pond. Medaka are hardy, need no heater, can overwinter outdoors, and breed readily, which makes them a good first breeding project.

What a line name means

Hobbyist line names describe color, fins, and scales as the breeder sees them. They are not a pedigree or a genetics test. The morph guide explains each one.
