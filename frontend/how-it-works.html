📋 Step by step

How Buying Works

What happens between checkout and the seller getting paid.

1
Pick a listing
Find what you want on the marketplace. Listings don't show a public location.
2
Pay by card
Checkout runs on Stripe Checkout. It's card only, no crypto. Sign in with email or Google to check out. Local-pickup orders can also check out as a guest with just an email.
Shipping orders: you get a live carrier quote for your address at checkout, plus a $2 handling fee.
3
We hold the payment
Your payment isn't sent to the seller yet. We hold it until the handoff or arrival is confirmed.
4
Handoff or delivery
Local pickup: meet the seller and give them your pickup code. It's on your order page and in the link in your order email. Shipping: the seller ships, and you confirm arrival when it comes in.
5
The seller gets paid
Local pickup: when the seller confirms the handoff with your code. Shipping: when you confirm arrival, or automatically once the buyer-protection window passes with no claim. Sellers are paid through Stripe Connect.

🛡️ Buyer protection

If Something Goes Wrong

For shipping orders. Local pickup is settled at the handoff, when the seller enters your code.

⏱️
Report within 48 hours
Report a problem within 48 hours of delivery. A seller may offer a longer window, never a shorter one.
  • At least 2 photos
  • A short description of the problem
⚖️
Seller first, then us
The seller has 24 hours to respond. After that, the claim comes to us for review.
  • Outcome is a refund, a replacement, or denied
📦
Shipping advice
Advice on heat or cold packs and timing is shown with the shipping rate. It's advice only. We don't enforce overnight shipping or weather holds.
🔨 Auctions

Auctions

Our fee is on the winning bid only. Clubs may add a buyer's premium of 0 to 25%, which goes to the club. It's set when the auction is created, and the club chooses whether the bidder or the club pays card processing.

⏳
Timed auctions
  • Bidding needs a card on file
  • A bid in the last 2 minutes extends the lot by 2 minutes
  • The winner's card is charged automatically when the lot closes
  • The money is held until pickup, then paid to the seller
🎟️
Club auction night
Clubs run the night with lots, paddles, a clerk and a checkout desk.
  • Pay at the desk with cash, a QR card payment, or a saved card
❓ Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what people ask before their first order.

Is real money moving yet? +

No. Aquacellum is in beta and Stripe is in test mode, so no real money moves yet.

Can I pay with crypto? +

No. Payments are card only, through Stripe Checkout.

Do I need an account to buy? +

For shipping orders, yes. Sign in with email or Google. Local-pickup orders can check out as a guest with just an email.

Where do I find my pickup code? +

On your order page, and through the link in your order email. The seller enters it at the handoff, and that releases the payment to them.

What if a shipped fish arrives dead or sick? +

Report it within 48 hours of delivery with at least 2 photos and a short description. The seller has 24 hours to respond, then it comes to us for review. The outcome is a refund, a replacement, or denied.

What if I never confirm arrival? +

The payment is released to the seller automatically once the buyer-protection window passes with no claim.

Is my location public? +

No. Listings have no public location. A pickup spot is shared only between the buyer and seller of that order.

How do sellers get paid? +

Through Stripe Connect. Stripe handles seller identity verification during onboarding.
